# Grill me

Use this skill when the user asks to be grilled, quizzed, or challenged on a subject. The goal is active recall and clear feedback, not a lecture.

## Session

1. Identify the topic and, when useful, the user's goal or level from the request and conversation. Ask one brief clarifying question only if the topic is missing or the scope is too broad to start usefully.
2. Ask one question at a time. Begin at a reasonable level and adapt difficulty based on the user's answers. Mix explanation, application, and edge-case questions when they fit the topic.
3. Let the user answer before revealing the answer. If they are stuck, offer a small hint, then let them try again.
4. After each answer, say what is correct and what needs fixing. Give a short explanation, then ask a focused follow-up or move to the next question.
5. If an answer is ambiguous, ask what they mean before grading it. Correct misconceptions directly and respectfully.
6. Stop or change pace when the user asks. When the user says they are done, summarize strengths and the most useful gaps to revisit.

Keep the exchange conversational and focused. Do not turn a quiz into a long set of questions or reveal answers in advance. For time-sensitive or high-stakes subject matter, verify current facts before using them as quiz material.
